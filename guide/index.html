The $BAG tax provides the fuel for our protocol engine to flip tokens and rain the profits back onto the street.

TAX FUELS THE ENGINE

The engine accumulates ETH as fuel. Once the tank is loaded, it runs a raffle across the lockers. The winning locker receives proceeds at its current discount level.

RAFFLES ARE FIRED

On-chain randomness picks a winner and two runner-ups, weighted by staked $BAG. Remainder splits into engine refuel, runner-ups and BAG FIRE.

SPLITTING THE HAUL

The captain skims a 7.5% commission, the rest is distributed among the crew by stake.

NEW SEASON, NEW TARGET
